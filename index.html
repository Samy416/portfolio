Why I Build AI Systems

Outside of work, I build systems that turn an idea into finished educational content and get it in front of an audience across social platforms, the kind of production and distribution work that used to need a full team behind it. What draws me to it isn't the technology for its own sake, it's what that automation makes possible for a creator or a small business without one.

The same thinking carries past content. Most businesses have some repetitive, multi-step process eating up time that could go toward actually growing, and I like finding that process and building the system that runs it, whether that's content, outreach, or day-to-day operations.
